We Build Systems
That Run.

Devistio is a production-focused Cloud & DevOps engineering company with deep hands-on expertise in designing, deploying, and operating AWS-native infrastructure at scale. We work across the full lifecycle: from Terraform modules and CI/CD pipelines to Kubernetes security hardening and observability stacks.

Founded by Talha Rehman, Devistio was built out of deep curiosity about how large-scale systems stay reliable under pressure. That curiosity became a company with expertise across cloud platforms, container orchestration, infrastructure-as-code, and the full observability stack.

"Our infrastructure ships with security built in, not bolted on. Every deployment is reproducible. Every system is observable. Every incident teaches the next iteration something new."

Devistio specialises in platform engineering and cloud security. We believe great infrastructure is invisible to end users: it just works, at any scale, under any load.
