I'm a production-focused DevOps and Cloud Engineer with hands-on experience designing, deploying, and operating AWS-native infrastructure at scale. I work across the full lifecycle — from Terraform modules and CI/CD pipelines to Kubernetes security hardening and observability stacks.

My journey into DevOps started with a deep curiosity about how large-scale systems stay reliable under pressure. That curiosity turned into expertise across cloud platforms, container orchestration, infrastructure-as-code, and the full observability stack.

"My infrastructure ships with security built in, not bolted on. Every deployment is reproducible. Every system is observable. Every incident teaches the next iteration something new."

When I'm not pushing Terraform plans or tuning Kubernetes workloads, I'm studying new patterns in platform engineering and cloud security. I believe great infrastructure is invisible to end users — it just works.
